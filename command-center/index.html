      Decision language

      PASS
      Decline execution; retain useful evidence.
      WATCH
      Wait for a specific price, event, or deadline.
      INVESTIGATE
      Resolve a material evidence gap.

      Requires the full workflow and human Final Gate. Placement still needs a receipt.
