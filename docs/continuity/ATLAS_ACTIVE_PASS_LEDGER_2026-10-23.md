1. COMEBACK: given score deficit D, regulation time left and as-of possessions remaining, how many additional relative points are necessary? 2. PRESSURE: what changed between halves in verified turnover event rate, offensive FGA opportunities, possession count, transition, free throws, offensive rebounds, shot location/contest and personnel? Record whether PBP offers the required denominators. Avoid attributing a loss to generic momentum or a defensive adjustment without observed shifts and alternatives.
3. AUSAR THOMPSON VS SHOOTER: distinguish defender on court, verified one-to-one defensive assignment, shots deterred per matchup possession, difficulty and conversion of shots actually attempted, and redistribution to teammate shooters/potential assists. Box score or ordinary PBP alone cannot prove assignment. Never infer a favorable shooter under solely from Ausar's reputation, steals or DFG%. Cross-check NBA Matchup DET FACTOR, tracking coverage and lineup participation if obtainable under allowed source usage.
4. CONDITIONAL PLAYER STATLINE: projected minutes x player chances per on-court possession x attempt or potential-assist/rebound opportunity x conversion, controlling for possession/usage conservation, foul trouble, matchups and teammate reallocation. Compare independent price-blind baseline and forecast at identical cutoff.
5. TOTALS: expected remaining combined points = expected A points plus B points from remaining possessions and conditional efficiency; the current scoreboard adds the known points. Slowdown does not guarantee an under, and comeback does not guarantee an over. No edge absent line, price, rule, timing, uncertainty and calibrated distribution.
