### Strongest anti-hype lesson

Moving past book prices into player totals demands a realistic source of minutes and opportunity, a statistical counting distribution (points, attempts, threes, rebounds, assists), and independent CALIBRATOR reliability tests. One open prop author reports points MAE 4.604 on a 2025-26 playoff holdout vs last-ten mean 4.728, and an under-only selected 131-59 record, but full training code, raw rows, executable book odds and independent ROI verification are unavailable. Treat these as promising METHOD descriptions, not copyable edges. Only a chronological, holdout-calibrated, reproducible result can graduate beyond research. Verify tails and dependence; no naive Normal or Poisson for every NBA count by default.

### Cappers are a SOCIAL INTELLIGENCE INPUT, not trusted forecasters

Paper: Brown & Reade (2019) 'The wisdom of amateur crowds', European Journal of Operational Research, DOI 10.1016/j.ejor.2018.07.015, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377221718306209 . The paper reports a historical 68,339-tip crowd-consensus analysis where aggregation carried information beyond odds, but selecting supposedly best individual tipsters by experience or accuracy did NOT improve return in their sample. This is not an NBA DraftKings validated strategy and cannot be imported as current profit.

Capper-admission contract under FILTER/VERIFY + EDGE THIEF: require original pre-event pick receipt, matchup, market/line, exact named accessible book and odds, immutable timestamp, stake, settlement, visible losses/abstentions, revision policy, true same-line/market closing reference and source permission. Compute ROI after vig and costs, game/market-level cluster uncertainty and CLV. Never call a high win rate proof of EV. Public site claimed ROI/CLV without independently verified selection/receipt universe gets SELF_REPORTED_NOT_VERIFIED. Market movement is informational but historical 2007/1998 NBA market-efficiency papers do not prove today's profitable public caps or a current under.
