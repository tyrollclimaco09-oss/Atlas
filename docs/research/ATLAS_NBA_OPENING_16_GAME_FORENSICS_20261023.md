## Limits and next proof gate

The complete score census is NOT 16 official PBP reconstructions. For each game next join action/event PBP against box: period-specific turnovers, FGA, FTA, offensive rebounds, actual possessions, shots/contests, substitutions, injuries, true on-court roles and where possible sourced defender assignments. Keep every missing and conflicting event. Build future as-of halftime and Q3 freezes, compare to earlier-only simple baseline, then score with proper metrics. No bets, no implied unders, no profitable edge, no Arc III signoffs.
